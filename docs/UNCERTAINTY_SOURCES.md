# Sourced measurement uncertainties (candidates for the sigma rule, issue #9)

Nothing here is applied to `config/features.yaml` except the two Barker 2021 midpoints that were already there. The sigma rule needs one number per column and body in the same unit as the column, and almost every source below states a different statistic (RMS, LE90, percent, calibration precision) at a different resolution. Turning one into the other is a decision for Anay and Vihaan, so each is listed with what it says. Checked 2026-11-13 by opening the pages; a quote marked SNIPPET was seen only in a search result and is a lead.

| Column / body | What a source says | Source | Why it is not applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| slope_med, slope_p90 / Moon | "typical RMS values of ~0.30-0.50 m and ~1.5-2.5 deg" (height and slope); one range for four south-pole sites, 5 m/pixel LDEMs | Barker et al. 2021, PSS 203:105119, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205009660 ; https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78 | already in the file as midpoints; figures are for 5 m pixels, not for slope at a 500 m baseline |
| relief / Moon | same height range; also "systematic errors on the order of 0.5 m are the dominant source of uncertainty" and reconstructions "better than 1 m radially" | Smith et al. 2017 (accepted manuscript), https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20160008718/downloads/20160008718.pdf | height error of a 500 m global grid not given |
| relief / Mars | "vertical accuracy of approx. one meter" for the best (about 1/16 deg) grid; range resolution 37.5 cm | Smith et al. 2001, JGR 106(E10):23689, https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20010002502 | RMS or LE90 not stated; the USGS 463 m DEM page says "total elevation uncertainty is at least +-3 m" |
| relief and slope / Earth, Copernicus GLO-30 | absolute vertical accuracy "< 4 m (90% linear error)"; relative vertical "< 2 m (slope <= 20%)", "< 4 m (slope > 20%)"; independent ICESat check RMSE 1.68 m, LE90 2.17 m | Copernicus DEM Product Handbook v5.0 (Nov 2022), https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/sites/default/files/media/files/2024-06/geo1988-copernicusdem-spe-002_producthandbook_i5.0.pdf ; EGU23-10090 | LE90 is not a sigma (a Gaussian 90% factor is 1.645, which is arithmetic, not a sourced choice); no slope error |
| relief and slope / Earth, NASADEM | SRTM specification: vertical error smaller than 16 m absolute at the 90% level, "generally smaller than eight meters in sparsely vegetated areas"; an `err` layer ships with the data (mm) | NASADEM User Guide V1.3, https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/2237/NASADEM_User_Guide_V13.pdf | the SRTM spec, not a NASADEM validation; no slope error |
| t_mean, t_coldest / Earth, MODIS LST | "within 1 C of the in situ measured thermal emission temperature (Wan, 2014)"; 0.4 +- 0.9 C at Greenland summit for cloud-filtered data; stage 2 validation | Scambos et al. 2018, https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/26979/noaa_26979_DS1.pdf ; https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-mod11a2-061 ; Wan C6 users' guide | accuracy of single retrievals, not of a 2015-2024 mean or coldest-month value |

| silica_index / Earth | ASTER GED: "average band error of ~1%"; 1-2% for mixed vegetation pixels | ASTER GED v4 user guide, https://cimss.ssec.wisc.edu/iremis/includes/docs/ASTER_GEDv4_UserGuide.pdf | the index formula is not defined, so the error cannot be propagated |
