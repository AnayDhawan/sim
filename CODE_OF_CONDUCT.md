# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- welcoming and inclusive language
- respect for differing viewpoints and experience
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it
- being honest about uncertainty, including our own

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- sexualised language or imagery
- trolling, insults and derogatory comments
- public or private harassment
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- misrepresenting data, sources or results

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **dhawansanay@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.
